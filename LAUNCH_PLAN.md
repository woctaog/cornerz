## Launch Communities (Priority Order)

### 1. r/webgames
- No self-promotion stigma — this subreddit exists exactly for this
- Post title: something like "I made a free Connections-style puzzle game where you can submit your own puzzles"
- Include a screenshot or short GIF of gameplay

### 2. r/nytconnections
- Huge, engaged audience already primed for this genre
- Angle: "I love Connections so much I built one where the community makes the puzzles"
- Do NOT lead with self-promotion — lead with the concept and let the game speak for itself
- Check subreddit rules before posting; some allow "I made this" posts

### 3. Hacker News — Show HN
- Format: `Show HN: Cornerz – a Connections-style puzzle game with community-submitted puzzles`
- The live validation UX and submission form will resonate with a technical audience
- Keep the post description short; let the link do the work

### 4. r/SideProject
- Secondary; easy effort for incremental reach
- Developer-friendly community that appreciates the craft

### 5. r/wordle (secondary)
- Similar demographic to Connections players
- Lower priority; only worth posting if early traction warrants it

## Key Messaging

**Lead with the submission angle**, not just "play my game." Most puzzle games ask you to play. Cornerz asks you to *create*. That's the hook.

Example framing:
> "The game launches with 12 puzzles I made myself, but the whole point is for the community to make them. There's a built-in submission form with a live preview so you can see your puzzle take shape as you build it."

**Spoiler-free sharing** is already built in (emoji share card). Encourage players to share their results — organic reach with zero effort.

---

## Content Sustainability Plan

- **Target**: maintain a 2–4 week puzzle backlog at all times
- Review submissions weekly; add approved puzzles to `puzzles.json`
- If submissions are slow early, have a few extra puzzles ready as a personal buffer
- Consider posting a reminder/update post after week 1 with early stats ("X people have played, here's how it's going") — this re-engages the community and invites more submissions

---

## Things to Avoid

- Don't post the same link across all communities on the same day — pace it out over a week
- Don't over-explain the rules in launch posts; a GIF says more than a paragraph
- Don't promise features you haven't built yet

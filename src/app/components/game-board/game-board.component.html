How To Play

  • Place 12 words on the outer edge of the 4x4 grid. The 4 center cells are disabled.
  • Complete 4 lines: top, bottom, left, and right. Each line must match exactly one category.
  • Corner words are shared between two categories and connect intersecting lines.
  • Use drag and drop, or tap a tile then tap a cell. You can swap by dropping on an occupied cell.
  • Correct lines lock as solved. Incorrect guesses return only newly placed tiles and increase mistakes.
  • Win when all 4 lines are solved.
